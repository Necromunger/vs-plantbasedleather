# Plant Fiber Hide

A plant-based alternative to animal hides that follows the vanilla Vintage Story
leatherworking process as closely as possible. Bamboo stands in for animal hides,
letting players craft leather and pelts without killing animals.

The mod adds only two items; Bamboo Pulp and the Soaked Plant Hide. Merging two
Soaked Plant Hides produces a genuine vanilla Large Soaked Hide, so everything from that point on is a vanilla item on the normal vanilla progression.

## Crafts

### Prep

```
5L Water + 5 Lime                          → 5L Limewater              (barrel, no seal)
5L Water + 2 Powdered Borax                → 5L Diluted Borax          (barrel, no seal)
10L Water + 1 Oak/Acacia Log               → 10L Weak Tannin           (barrel, seal 24h)
10L Weak Tannin + 1 Oak/Acacia Log         → 10L Strong Tannin         (barrel, seal 24h)
```

Diluted Borax may replace Limewater in any soak below; it seals in 18h instead of 20h. (vanilla)

### 2 Leather - Medium Hide route

```
3 Bundles of bamboo stakes                 → 6 Bamboo Pulp             (quern, 1 spare)
5L Limewater + 5 Bamboo Pulp               → 1 Soaked Plant Hide       (barrel, seal 20h)
1 Soaked Plant Hide + Knife                → 1 Medium Scraped Hide     (grid or ground)
1 Medium Scraped Hide + 4L Weak Tannin     → 1 Medium Prepared Hide    (barrel, seal 72h)
1 Medium Prepared Hide + 4L Strong Tannin  → 2 Leather                 (barrel, seal 108h)
```

### 3 Leather - Large Hide route

```
5 Bundles of bamboo stakes                 → 10 Bamboo Pulp            (quern)
10L Limewater + 10 Bamboo Pulp             → 2 Soaked Plant Hides      (barrel, seal 20h)
2 Soaked Plant Hides                       → 1 Large Soaked Hide       (grid, no tool)
1 Large Soaked Hide + Knife                → 1 Large Scraped Hide      (grid or ground)
1 Large Scraped Hide + 6L Weak Tannin      → 1 Large Prepared Hide     (barrel, seal 72h)
1 Large Prepared Hide + 6L Strong Tannin   → 3 Leather                 (barrel, seal 108h)
```

### 3 Sturdy Leather

```
5 Bundles of bamboo stakes                 → 10 Bamboo Pulp            (quern)
10L Limewater + 10 Bamboo Pulp             → 2 Soaked Plant Hides      (barrel, seal 20h)
2 Soaked Plant Hides                       → 1 Large Soaked Hide       (grid, no tool)
1 Large Soaked Hide + Knife                → 1 Large Scraped Hide      (grid or ground)
1 Large Scraped Hide + 3L Chromium Sulfate → 1 Large Chromium Hide     (cooking pot)
1 Large Chromium Hide + 3 Beeswax          → 3 Sturdy Leather          (cooking pot)
```

### 2 Pelts

```
5 Bundles of bamboo stakes                 → 10 Bamboo Pulp            (quern)
10L Limewater + 10 Bamboo Pulp             → 2 Soaked Plant Hides      (barrel, seal 20h)
2 Soaked Plant Hides + 1 Rendered Fat      → 2 Medium Oiled Hides      (grid)
2 Medium Oiled Hides                       → 2 Medium Pelts            (cures 48h)
```

### Bulk soaking

```
25 Bundles of bamboo stakes                → 50 Bamboo Pulp            (quern)
50L Limewater + 50 Bamboo Pulp             → 10 Soaked Plant Hides     (barrel, seal 20h)
```
